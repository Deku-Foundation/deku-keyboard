  Wikidata Lexemes, Arabic (Q13955), CC0 1.0, https://www.wikidata.org/

Changes in Deku:
  The frequency list is bundled as an offline, read-only Android asset and used to create a
  two-character prefix index for on-device candidate lookup. No source sentences are distributed.
  This dictionary is an MSA/newswire baseline, not an Algerian dialect lexicon, not a complete
  morphological dictionary, and not a claim of comprehensive Arabic spell-checking.

The upstream project's ATTRIBUTION.md and sources.json identify the frequency list as CC BY 4.0
and state that the specific Leipzig download corpora were confirmed with the corpus provider on
2026-08-04 as usable under that licence, including commercial use. The source project and its
licence metadata are pinned above so the data chain is auditable.
